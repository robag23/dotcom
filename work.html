EXPERIENCE

Different roles. A consistent interest in how work actually gets done.

My experience crosses emergency response, entrepreneurship, frontline supervision and public service coordination. I see those roles as a continuous development of responsibility, judgement and organisational understanding.

Emergency response

2009–2020

Entrepreneurship

Running my own taxi and private-hire business made responsibility practical: customers, scheduling, finances, service delivery and everyday commercial decisions.

2017–2025

Community Payback

Supervisory work developed my experience of people management, site operations, health and safety, risk assessment and planning.

2025–

Placement coordination

My current work brings together operational planning, stakeholder communication, risk and coordination.
