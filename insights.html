DBA Research

IN DEVELOPMENT

Beyond AI Adoption: Organisational Capabilities as Drivers of AI-Enabled Business Value in UK SMEs

The doctoral research asks why one SME can create meaningful business value from AI while another, even with access to similar tools, cannot. The focus moves beyond adoption itself to the organisational capabilities that sit between AI use and business value.

Working Paper No. 1 provides the conceptual starting point; the doctoral research is intended to test, refine and develop that thinking empirically.
