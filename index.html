Current research

Beyond AI Adoption

Why similar AI tools produce different organisational outcomes.

Read more →

My research examines why organisations using similar AI technologies often achieve very different outcomes. The difference is rarely explained by the tool alone.

The problem behind the title

AI adoption is often measured through access, implementation or usage. Those measures say little about whether the organisation is learning, changing decisions or creating sustainable value.

What I am exploring

The study focuses on leadership capability, organisational readiness, organisational learning, dynamic capabilities, absorptive capacity, innovation capability and knowledge sharing within UK SMEs.

Current position

The Research Proposal and conceptual framework are complete enough to guide further development. The next stage is strengthening the literature base and preparing the work for empirical testing and publication.

Research journey

The journey is part of the evidence

Notes, milestones and learning shared openly.

This platform documents the development of my thinking, not only polished outcomes. That includes questions, revisions, unsuccessful applications and the gradual strengthening of a research direction.

Why document the process?

Research rarely develops in a straight line. Recording the process makes it possible to see which observations shaped the model, which assumptions changed and how practical experience became a researchable question.

Milestones already reached

First Class BSc, MBA with Distinction, Research Proposal, ORCID, Google Scholar profile, Capability-to-Value Model, Working Paper No. 1 and a growing LinkedIn Research Series.

What comes next

First academic publication, continued doctoral applications, research collaboration and eventual empirical validation of the model.
