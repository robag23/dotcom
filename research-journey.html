Research Journey • Research Insight

One question led to another.

How my interest moved from improving AI tools to understanding the organisational capabilities that turn AI into sustainable value.

25 July 20266 min readResearch development

I began with a technology-focused question: how can AI become better?

The more I read, the less complete that question felt. Organisations could access similar tools, yet their outcomes were very different. Some moved beyond experimentation and embedded AI into everyday work. Others remained at the level of isolated pilots.

The question changed

The more useful question became: how can organisations become better at creating value with AI?

This shift moved my attention towards leadership, organisational learning, readiness, innovation capability and the ability to adapt continuously. Technology remained important, but it was no longer the full explanation.

Why this mattered

That change became the foundation of my current research direction and the Capability-to-Value Model. The model begins with AI availability and adoption, but places organisational capability at the centre of value creation.

Where the journey goes next

The next stage is disciplined research: strengthening the literature base, refining the conceptual relationships and testing whether the framework explains the experience of UK SMEs.
