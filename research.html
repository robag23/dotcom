RESEARCH DIRECTION

Beyond AI Adoption.

My research is not primarily about whether SMEs adopt AI. It asks why some organisations turn AI into meaningful business value while others, even with access to similar technologies, remain at the level of experimentation.

THE CORE RESEARCH THEME

Beyond AI Adoption: Organisational Capabilities as Drivers of AI-Enabled Business Value in UK SMEs

AI adoption is not an outcome in itself. An organisation may use AI in marketing, customer service, analytics, administration, decision support or generative tasks without necessarily becoming more productive, innovative or competitive.

The research therefore focuses on what happens between AI adoption and business value. Its starting proposition is that the difference may be explained less by the technology itself and more by organisational capabilities, how they interact, and whether AI becomes genuinely integrated into organisational practice.

CENTRAL QUESTION

Why can one SME create real business value from AI while another — even with access to similar AI tools — cannot?

CAPABILITY-TO-VALUE MODEL (CVM)

AI Availability → AI Adoption → Organisational Capabilities → AI Integration → Business Value → Sustainable Advantage

The Capability-to-Value Model gives structure to the idea that access to and use of AI are not the same as value creation. The next step is to make these relationships empirically testable and refine the model through doctoral research.

Leadership capability

strategic direction, decisions and support

Organisational readiness

preparedness for change and AI integration

Organisational learning

how the organisation learns from use and experience

Absorptive capacity

recognising, absorbing and applying new knowledge

Dynamic capabilities

adapting and reconfiguring resources

Innovation capability

turning AI opportunities into new processes and solutions

Knowledge sharing

sharing knowledge across people, teams and functions

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

The planned research question structure

RQ1: How do UK SMEs translate AI adoption into business value?

RQ2: Which organisational capabilities influence the successful integration of AI within UK SMEs?

RQ3: How do leadership capability and organisational readiness influence AI-enabled business value creation?

RQ4: What role do organisational learning, absorptive capacity and innovation capability play in converting AI adoption into business outcomes?

RQ5: How can these relationships be integrated into a capability-based framework for AI-enabled business value creation in SMEs?

GENERATIVE AI

An important contemporary context, but not the boundary of the whole research programme.

Generative AI remains an important part of the research, particularly for questions of organisational readiness, leadership and learning. The wider research direction, however, is deliberately broader: AI-enabled business value and organisational capability.

This allows the research to remain relevant beyond a single technology category or current generation of tools, while Generative AI continues to provide a concrete and timely context for focused studies.
