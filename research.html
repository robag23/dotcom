Beyond AI Adoption.

How organisational capabilities turn AI access into sustainable business value in UK SMEs.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Beyond AI adoption: what allows organisations to turn technology into sustainable value?

My research interest sits between technology and organisational capability. AI may be available and increasingly powerful, but availability does not automatically produce business value. I am interested in what happens between adoption and outcome.

The working direction focuses on UK SMEs and the organisational capabilities that may influence whether AI becomes embedded in useful, sustainable ways.

01

Research question

How do leadership, organisational readiness, learning, innovation, dynamic capabilities and absorptive capacity shape AI-enabled business value?

02

Capability-to-Value Model

The developing CVM links AI availability and adoption with organisational capabilities, integration, business value and sustainable advantage.
